Delete your Enkirai account

Last updated 2026-11-25

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Enkirai and go to Settings.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account, plan, codes and usage counts are deleted from our server right away. Conversations saved on your phone are deleted too.

Without the app

Email hello@enkirai.com with the subject "Delete my Enkirai account" and the Account ID shown in Settings → Account (if you can still see it). We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: the device account, plan, redeemed codes, usage counts and feedback linked to it.
  • Kept: anonymous crash reports (no account link) for up to 12 months, and records we must keep by law, such as payment records held by Google.

To also stop a paid subscription, cancel it in Google Play → Payments and subscriptions.
