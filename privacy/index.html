Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-11-25

Enkirai is a conversation assistant for Android made by m.labs (Marius Žemaitis, Lithuania), who is the data controller. Questions or requests: hello@enkirai.com.

The short version: your transcripts, notes and tasks stay on your phone. To work, Enkirai sends the audio of the conversation to a speech-to-text service and short pieces of the text to an AI model, only while you have it switched on. We do not show ads and we do not sell data.

Legal bases (GDPR): performance of the service you request (art. 6(1)(b)), our legitimate interest in keeping the service working and safe (art. 6(1)(f)), and your consent for the waitlist and for optional permissions (art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time.

Service providers

  • Soniox: speech-to-text and voice for tips.
  • Anthropic (Claude): tips, answers, web lookups and notes. Data sent through the API is not used to train their models.
  • Supabase (servers in the EU, Frankfurt): device account, usage counts, codes, crash reports and the waitlist.
  • Google: app distribution and, later, payments through Google Play.

Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Such transfers are protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent safeguards.

Recording other people

Enkirai processes the voices of everyone in the conversation. You are responsible for telling the people you talk to that you use an AI note taker and for following the law where you are. The app shows clearly when it is listening.

How long we keep data

  • On your phone: until you delete a conversation or uninstall the app.
  • On our server: account, plan and usage counts while the account exists; crash reports up to 12 months; waitlist until you ask us to remove you or until launch plus 12 months.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, object to processing, or withdraw consent, by writing to hello@enkirai.com. You can delete your account in the app (Settings → Account → Delete account) or as described on the account deletion page. You can also complain to the Lithuanian State Data Protection Inspectorate (vdai.lrv.lt).

Children

Enkirai is not meant for children under 16.

Changes

We will update this page when the app changes how it handles data, and note the date at the top.
